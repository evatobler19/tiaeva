Fiesta de la Masa Vienesa

The Fiesta de la Masa Vienesa is a traditional festival held in Villa General Belgrano. It celebrates Central European pastry and baking traditions, with cakes, strudels, pastries and homemade bread as the main attractions.

Summerfest

Summerfest is Villa General Belgrano's traditional summer festival. It takes place during January and February and includes live music, traditional dances, food and outdoor activities in a fun and family-friendly atmosphere.

Oktoberfest

Oktoberfest is the most famous festival in Villa General Belgrano and is known as the National Beer Festival. It celebrates Central European traditions with music, food, dances and cultural activities.
